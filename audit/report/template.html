1

Ads are buying customers at three times break-even

Last 90 days: $13,723 in ad spend ($9,473 Meta, $4,250 Google) and 78 new customers from every source combined. That's $176 per new customer against your $90 red line. At this spend the store needs about $22,600 a month in sales to break even; it averaged $11,372.

2

95% of shoppers never add to cart

Add-to-cart rate fell from 13.2% of sessions in March to 4.7% in November. On a phone, the Rewards button sits on top of the price or the Add to Cart button on product pages. Fix: the product page redesign below, starting with the Rewards button (15 minutes in the app settings).
