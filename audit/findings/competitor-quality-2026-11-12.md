# Competitor quality, shipping and holiday-promo check (US, as of 12 Nov 2026)

Companion to `/home/user/zeotein-theme/audit/findings/pricing-research.md` (prices as of 10 Nov). This file adds the quality side, re-checks shipping terms, re-checks only the prices that file marked [U] or that now look different, and logs holiday promos.

## Method and labels

- **WebFetch was blocked.** kachava.com and huel.com both failed DNS ("outside the session's allowed network domains"), so I stopped trying other hosts. Every fact below comes from **WebSearch result snippets** (about 60 queries). Crawl dates are not shown and some indexed pages are stale.
- **[B]** = stated on the brand's own domain (including its help center, PDFs and forum staff posts).
- **[3P]** = third party: a retailer listing that reproduces the label, a review site, a coupon or deal site, or a certifier's own directory (NSF, Informed Sport). Certifier directories are the authoritative record for a certification, but they are still [3P].
- **[U]** = unconfirmed, conflicting or possibly stale. Treat as directional.
- **"derived"** = my arithmetic from stated figures, not a published number.
- Testing claims are recorded as each brand states them. Nothing was inferred.
- The chocolate flavor was used wherever one exists.

## Observations for the audit (facts only; run any copy through the compliance scrub)

- **Zeotein is the only chocolate product in this set with no sweetener.** Stevia is the most common sweetener (Huel, Ancient Nutrition, Equip, Vital, Bulletproof, Perfect Keto, KOS, Orgain). Monk fruit is next (Ka'Chava, Paleovalley, Primal Kitchen, Truvani, KOS, Ritual). Ka'Chava, Huel and KOS also add 2–4 g of coconut sugar or nectar.
- **Recognized third-party certifications sit with Ka'Chava (NSF Certified for Sport), Vital Proteins (NSF Certified for Sport), Ritual (Informed Sport + Clean Label Project), Huel (NSF 229) and KOS (Non-GMO Project).** Truvani, KOS and Orgain are USDA Organic. The bone-broth and collagen analogs (Ancient Nutrition, Paleovalley, Equip, Bulletproof, Perfect Keto, Primal Kitchen) rely on self-stated testing. Zeotein's testing claims were not supplied.
- **Grass-fed is standard among the animal-protein peers** (Ancient Nutrition, Paleovalley, Equip, Vital, Bulletproof, Perfect Keto). Primal Kitchen dropped the claim because it could not document it, which shows that retailers ask for proof of grass-fed sourcing.
- **Closest analogs (collagen + MCT or coconut):** Perfect Keto (10 g collagen + 5 g MCT, stevia), Bulletproof Chocolate (20 g collagen + MCT, erythritol and stevia) and Primal Kitchen (11 g collagen + coconut milk, monk fruit). All deliver less protein than Zeotein's 23 g, and none combines bone broth with added collagen.
- **Heavy-metal testing is now a common claim** after Consumer Reports' Oct 2025 lead study, which named Huel Black Edition and older Equip Chocolate lots. KOS (batch COA tool) and Truvani (test-results page) publish results. Any Zeotein claim here must avoid detox or "binding" language for zeolite.
