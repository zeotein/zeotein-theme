# Bulk tiers, the Protein Blends page and product descriptions: plan (Nov 14, 2026)

## Where the new product pages live
They are the existing product pages, not new ones: Shop → Zeotein Cacao → Bulk bag / 30 sticks / 7-Day, plus the pages Google Shopping, ads and search already send people to. Nothing new appears in the menu. The format tiles join the three Cacao formats together.

## Switching formats: separate pages, made to feel like one
Each format is its own product with its own inventory, subscription plans, reviews, photos and description ("Two scoops…" on the bag, "Tear, pour, shake" on sticks, "A week of Zeotein" on the 7-Day). Merging them into one product with a format option would break Google Shopping links, ads, existing subscriptions and review history, so don't. The tiles now open the next page at the same spot, pre-loaded, so only the content changes. A true swap-in-place is for the Protein Blends page below.

Why not separate "3-bag bundle" products: duplicate pages and SEO, reviews split three ways, more SKUs and Seal plans to manage, and Google Shopping would show near-duplicate listings.

## Protein Blends page (Rafael's concept, done the safe way)
One page, "Protein Blends" (Shop → Protein Blends), with a single buy box where only the selected item changes in place:
1. **Blend:** Cacao | Vegan
2. **Format:** Bulk bag (Cacao only) | 30 sticks | 7-Day
3. **How many:** 1 / 2 / 3 (not for 7-Day)
4. **Subscribe & Save | One-time:** price, per serving, renewal line, gifts

It reuses the lander's tested buy-box logic (tiers, plans, gifts). The product pages stay as the deep pages for Google Shopping, ads and search; Shopping can only send traffic to product pages.

## Product descriptions: one structure on every blend page

2. In every serving: 23g protein, 14g collagen peptides, 140 calories, 6 ingredients (format-specific serving: 2 scoops / 1 stick)

4. What's in your first order (gifts) and the 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee (first order, one supply)
5. Taste and mixing
