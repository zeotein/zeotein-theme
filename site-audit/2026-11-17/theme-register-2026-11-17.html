Theme rules

  1. Shopify allows 20 themes. At 20, nobody can make a working copy or a backup, so edits stall.
  2. Name every copy with what it is for and the date, for example "Backup before Black Friday page 20 Nov". "Copy of..." and "New Theme" tell nobody what is inside.
  3. One rollback backup at a time. When a new backup of the live theme is made, the older one goes on the delete list below.
  4. A working copy goes once its changes are live and checked, after a short safety window.
  5. Publish the Black Friday theme at 12:00 am CT on 27 Nov, not earlier. Its "30% off" copy would show before the storewide discount starts.
  6. When switching to a new theme, app embeds do not carry over. Judge.me and every other app embed must be on in the new theme before it goes live, or reviews disappear from the site. The Black Friday theme was checked on 17 Nov: same embeds as live.
  7. Before deleting, check this page, then update it the same day.

How each theme was checked

Shopify keeps a fingerprint of every file in every theme. Each theme's page layouts, sections, snippets, settings, scripts and images were compared against the live theme and the other themes; JSON settings files were compared by content. A theme counts as safe to delete only when every file that matters also exists, unchanged, in a theme that is being kept. On 17 Nov, Gmail and Drive were also searched for preview links and mentions of each theme; none pointed at a theme then in the library.
