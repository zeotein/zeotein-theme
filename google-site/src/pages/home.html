Questions, answered

Good Questions. Straight Answers.

What is Zeotein?

Protein blends and single-ingredient powders with short ingredient lists. Zeotein Cacao and Zeotein Vegan put protein and the rest of your shelf into one serving; the singles let you add one ingredient at a time.

What's the difference between Cacao and Vegan?

Cacao has 23g protein from grass-fed beef bone broth and grass-fed collagen peptides, with organic fermented cacao. Vegan has 20g plant protein from organic pumpkin seed and organic hemp in single-serve stick packs, with a natural nutty flavor.

How do I use it?

Shake a serving with 8 to 12 oz of water, milk or coffee, or blend it into a smoothie. It takes about 10 seconds.
