Zeotein Cacao, answered

What is Zeotein Cacao?

A six-ingredient cacao protein powder: grass-fed beef bone broth protein, grass-fed collagen peptides, organic fermented cacao, organic MCT oil powder, organic sea moss and a naturally occurring volcanic mineral. Each serving has 23g protein, 14g collagen peptides, 140 calories and zero sugar.

What is the volcanic mineral?

A naturally occurring mineral formed from volcanic ash, finely milled. Its full name is listed on the Supplement Facts panel printed on every bag. Questions about any ingredient? Email {{email}} and a real person will answer.

How do I make it?

One serving is two scoops in 10 to 12 oz of water, milk or coffee. Shake for about ten seconds, or blend it into a smoothie. A banana or frozen berries makes it creamier.
