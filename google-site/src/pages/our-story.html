Our story

Protein first. The rest of the shelf, built in.

Most protein routines turn into a shelf: a protein tub, a collagen tub, an add-on or two, each with its own scoop and its own label to decode. Zeotein puts protein first and builds the rest into one serving, with an ingredient list short enough to read in seconds.

What we make

Two formulas. One idea.

Zeotein Cacao is built on grass-fed beef bone broth protein and grass-fed collagen peptides, with organic fermented cacao, organic MCT and organic sea moss. Six ingredients, 23g protein, zero sugar.

Zeotein Vegan is built on organic pumpkin seed and organic hemp protein. Five ingredients, 20g plant protein, zero added sugar, in single-serve stick packs.

Both formulas include a naturally occurring volcanic mineral, named in full on the Supplement Facts panel printed on every bag and pack.

How we decide

Every ingredient has to earn its place

Three rules we keep for every formula.

01

Short enough to read

Six ingredients in Cacao, five in Vegan. If you can't read the whole list in a few seconds, it's too long.

02

Nothing added to sweeten it

No sweeteners and no whey. Cacao tastes closer to dark chocolate than a candy bar, and Vegan tastes nutty. That's on purpose.

03

Made for real life

A bag with a scoop that clips on, or stick packs that ride in a bag. Ten seconds in a shaker either way.
