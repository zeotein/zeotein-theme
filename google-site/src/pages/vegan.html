What is Zeotein Vegan?

A five-ingredient plant protein: organic pumpkin seed protein, organic hemp protein, organic MCT oil powder, organic sea moss and a naturally occurring volcanic mineral. Each stick has 20g plant protein, 170 calories and zero added sugar.

How do I mix it?

One stick in 10 to 12 oz of water, milk or a milk alternative. Shake it in a shaker or blend it into a smoothie.

What is the volcanic mineral?

A naturally occurring mineral formed from volcanic ash, finely milled. Its full name is listed on the Supplement Facts panel printed on every pack. Questions about any ingredient? Email {{email}}.
