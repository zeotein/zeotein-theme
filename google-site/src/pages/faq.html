The formulas

What's the difference between Cacao and Vegan?

Zeotein Cacao uses grass-fed beef bone broth protein and grass-fed collagen peptides: six ingredients, 23g protein, 14g collagen peptides, 140 calories and zero sugar per serving. It comes in a 30-serving bulk bag.

Zeotein Vegan uses organic pumpkin seed and organic hemp protein: five ingredients, 20g plant protein, 170 calories and zero added sugar per serving. It comes in single-serve stick packs.

What's in Zeotein Cacao?

Grass-fed beef bone broth protein, grass-fed collagen peptides, organic fermented cacao (raw, never alkalized), organic MCT oil powder, organic sea moss and a naturally occurring volcanic mineral. That's the whole list.

What's in Zeotein Vegan?

Organic pumpkin seed protein, organic hemp protein, organic MCT oil powder, organic sea moss and a naturally occurring volcanic mineral. That's the whole list.

What is the volcanic mineral?

A naturally occurring mineral formed from volcanic ash, finely milled. Its full name is listed on the Supplement Facts panel printed on every bag and pack. If you'd like to know more about any ingredient, email {{email}}.

Is there sugar or sweetener in it?

Zeotein Cacao has zero sugar and no sweeteners. Zeotein Vegan has zero added sugar and no artificial sweeteners. Cacao tastes like dark chocolate and Vegan tastes nutty, because nothing is added to cover them up.

Is it gluten-free, dairy-free or vegan?

Both formulas are gluten-free and dairy-free. Cacao is also soy-free, and its protein comes from beef bone broth and bovine collagen, so it is not vegan. Zeotein Vegan is fully plant-based.

Where can I see the full Supplement Facts?

The complete Supplement Facts panel is printed on every bag and stick pack. Key numbers per serving are on the Cacao and Vegan pages.

Making it

How do I make Zeotein Cacao?

One serving is two scoops in 10 to 12 oz of water, milk or coffee. Shake for about ten seconds or blend into a smoothie. A banana or frozen berries makes it creamier.

How do I make Zeotein Vegan?

One serving is one stick pack in 10 to 12 oz of water, milk or a milk alternative. Shake it or blend it into a smoothie.

How does it taste?

Cacao is cacao-forward and unsweetened, closer to dark chocolate than a candy-bar shake. Vegan has a natural nutty flavor from pumpkin seed and hemp. The recipe guides that come with the Starter Kits have plenty of ways to dress either one up.
